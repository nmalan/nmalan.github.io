AI-assisted literature review workflow

A workflow that uses AI to widen and interrogate your reading without replacing it. Steps 1, 2, 4 and 5 are the usual process; steps 3 and 6 are where AI adds value.

  1. Search as usual Human

    Start with the reference lists of papers you already know, and browse Google Scholar.

  2. Start a Zotero collection and download the PDFs Human

    Create a collection for the topic in Zotero and add the papers you have found, with their PDFs.

  3. Ask an AI to find connected papers AI

    Give the AI of your choice your reference list and your topic, and ask it to find connected papers, ranked by importance. Require it to cross-check every DOI.

  4. Download all the PDFs Human

    This is the manual step, although Zotero can retrieve some PDFs for you.

  5. Skim the papers as usual Human
  6. Interrogate your knowledge in NotebookLM AI

    Put all the PDFs into a NotebookLM collection and question it about the literature you have gathered.

AI tools can invent references. Check that each DOI points to the paper the AI says it does before adding it to your collection.

Writing

How to Write Backwards
Magnusson, W.E. (1996). Bulletin of the Ecological Society of America, 77(2), 88. DOI

A one-page guide to drafting a paper or thesis chapter in reverse: write the conclusions first, then only the results needed to support them, the methods needed to understand those results, a discussion limited to what modifies, extends or contradicts the conclusions, and finally a short introduction that poses the questions the conclusions answer.

Neil Malan  ·  Climate Change Research Centre, UNSW Sydney
